### Software Security Threat Modeling Report — E-commerce

- **Category:** Document
- **Period:** 2025–2026
- **Context:** Academic group project

**One-sentence description:** An academic group report assessing five e-commerce features through DFD-based STRIDE analysis, with OWASP references.

**Detailed description:** Our team assessed payment, search, promotions, login and registration, and cash on delivery, considering trade-offs between proposed mitigations. I handled the cash-on-delivery analysis and wrote Chapter 6, covering conclusions and recommendations. My analysis connected fake accounts, shared-device sessions, and weak logging to identity verification, session controls, and transaction audit logs. On 3 June 2026, the team also demonstrated SQL injection and authentication bypass using Burp Suite in an OWASP Juice Shop training lab.

**My role:** Cash-on-delivery threat analysis and the conclusions and recommendations chapter.

**Tools and methods:** Data flow diagrams, STRIDE, and OWASP references. Team training lab: OWASP Juice Shop and Burp Suite.

**Result / impact:** The report documents COD recommendations for OTP verification, device fingerprinting, auto-logout, primary-device confirmation, and IP, time, and device audit logs with OTP confirmation. My takeaway was that transaction traceability depends on identity, session controls, and logs.

**Scope:** The mitigations were analytical recommendations. The Juice Shop exercise was a training lab, not a live platform assessment.
